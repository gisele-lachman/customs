Unwritten rules, real stakes

Who has it

  • Business travelers meeting a client’s team
  • Wedding guests
  • Families meeting a partner’s parents
  • Students abroad
  • Hosts welcoming visitors

How they cope today

  • Piece it together from sites that don’t agree
  • Ask a friend who once lived there
  • Just guess

What a mistake costs

  • Embarrassment
  • A host who feels disrespected
  • Trust that takes a long time to rebuild
  • Sometimes, a lost deal

Limits and what’s next

Limits

  • Strict check can drop a true tip (Lagos)
  • Many kept tips only “partly supported”
  • Thin coverage on some topics (Nauru)
  • Some country facts missing or dated
  • Tested on one Mac; outages simulated
  • Trial: missed Japanese naming and forms of address

Next

  • Flippable cards with Wikipedia’s own sources
  • One retry when a quote is slightly off
  • More sources than Wikipedia
  • More languages
  • A real pilot with travelers and hosts
